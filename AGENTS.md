# Campaign working agreements

These instructions apply to every D&D campaign in this workspace, including new campaigns. Read `CLAUDE.md` for the existing DM workflow. The user's current instructions determine tone and override generic campaign-generation defaults.

## Shared daily play budget

- Before any D&D response, including setup, character work, rules discussions, or a new campaign, run `python3 /home/l/dnd_campaigns/tools/play_time.py begin --campaign <campaign>`. At the end of the response, run `finish` just before the final answer. Use `status` at least once per minute during sustained work.
- All campaigns share `/home/l/dnd_campaigns/campaigns/_shared/play-time.json`. Never create a separate allowance for a new character, campaign, chat, or session.
- The allowance is 3,600 seconds Monday through Friday and 7,200 seconds Saturday and Sunday, using America/Chicago dates. It resets at local midnight. Unused time does not carry forward.
- Count observed wall time spent handling D&D prompts, including tools, preparation, and saves. Exclude the player's reading, thinking, and time between messages. Exact model processing time is unavailable: record measured response intervals and label estimates for opening/final generation separately. Do not present estimates as exact measurements.
- At 10 minutes remaining, give one brief warning; at 2 minutes, checkpoint the current work. If the allowance expires during an accepted task, finish that task, including necessary verification, saves, and the final response. Count all work past the limit in the shared ledger. A task is the accepted request and the work needed to complete it, not the entire campaign or new requests. Once the task finishes, decline new D&D prompts until the next America/Chicago midnight. Do not introduce extra hooks or unrelated campaign work after completing the task.
- Before accepting a new task, check the shared budget. If it is already exhausted, give only a brief status and next reset time. A pending accepted task may still be completed under the rule above. Ordinary unrelated tasks remain available. A routine request for another turn or campaign does not reset or extend the budget.
- An open timer from another response must be resolved before beginning. Never charge the player's idle time by assuming a stale timer remained active. Reconstruct its end from known response/checkpoint timestamps; flag any unknown interval honestly.
- This is an assistant workflow safeguard, not an operating-system or account lock. Do not claim account-wide enforcement. Chats and devices that cannot read these instructions and the ledger cannot be metered here.

- In-game downtime does not consume real weeks by default. The world advances through agreed fictional time, not the player's absence. Use completed arcs, breathing room, and satisfying stopping points rather than endless cliffhangers. Let choices change long-term plans.
